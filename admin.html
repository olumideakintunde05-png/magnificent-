Home page sections

Change what visitors see on the home page: Popular Locations, Browse by Type, Why Choose Us, Amenities and the Trust badges & numbers at the top.

Hero video & photos

Hero video

A short video that plays silently on a loop behind the title at the top of the home page. When a video is saved it plays instead of the photos below. The photos still show while the video loads, and for guests who have data saver on. Best results: MP4 (H.264), landscape, 10–20 seconds, under 40 MB.

Hero photos

The big photos at the very top of the home page. They fade from one to the next in the order below. Use wide/landscape photos, up to 8.

Popular locations

Each location is a photo tile on the home page. Tapping it shows the properties in that area (the name should match the property's Location, e.g. “Lekki Phase 1”).

Browse by type

Each tile filters the properties by their Type. “Matches property type” must be written exactly like the Type on your properties (e.g. Studio, 2-Bedroom, Penthouse).

“Just booked” pop-up

Shows visitors real bookings as they come in — only a first name, last initial and area (for example “Fatima A. booked a stay in Lekki Phase 1”). Cancelling a booking removes it from the pop-up. If there are no bookings, nothing is shown.

Photo uploads

Photos you pick from your phone are uploaded to your Cloudinary account when these two are filled in. Leave them empty to use Firebase Storage.

Booking alerts by email

Every time a guest books, an email is sent to your admin address with the apartment, dates, guest name, phone and payment status. Press the button to send yourself a test email and check that it arrives (look in Spam too).

Google (SEO)

The sitemap is the list of pages you give Google so it finds every apartment. The one in the website already lists the original apartments. After you add or delete apartments, make a fresh one here, then upload it to GitHub (replace sitemap.xml) and resubmit it in Google Search Console.
