Booking Policy

Last updated: October 2026

These are the booking terms shown on every Shortlet Magnificent invoice. By paying an invoice, the guest confirms they have reviewed and accepted the apartment details, booking dates, guest information, total amount payable and these terms.

Our service to you

We are your booking agency and trusted link to shortlet apartments in Lagos. We facilitate reservations in carefully selected and inspected apartments that are independently owned or operated by property owners and operators. We do not own or directly operate the apartments we list. Our service is to show you suitable accommodation options, confirm availability, facilitate your reservation, and remain available to support you throughout your booking and stay.

As each apartment is independently owned or operated, on-site facilities, maintenance, cleaning, building management, and day-to-day property operations are handled by the relevant property or facility management team, not by us. However, you are never left without support. If anything during your stay requires attention, we remain available to assist with communication, coordination, follow-up, and escalation with the relevant property team.

Cancellation policy

Our general policy is that a 100% cancellation charge applies to confirmed bookings. This means the accommodation amount paid is not refundable once the booking is confirmed and the booked dates cannot be changed.

Some apartments have their own cancellation terms, and a booking may occasionally warrant an exceptional review. Where that applies, the terms for that property or booking will be communicated to you in writing before payment or at the time of review, and those terms will apply to your booking.

Property-specific cancellation terms: Will be communicated if any.

Refundable caution / security deposit

A caution deposit is required for every booking and is subject to the following terms:

  • The deposit is refundable, subject to the property’s post-stay inspection report confirming no damage, missing items, violated rules, or other chargeable issues attributable to the guest.
  • The inspection and assessment of any deductions are carried out by the property owner, operator, or management. Shortlet Magnificent facilitates communication and follows up where necessary. We do not control this process.
  • Where a deduction is required, the applicable amount will be determined by the facility management based on verified costs.
  • Refunds are typically processed within 24–72 hours after check-out, subject to the property’s inventory process.
  • If verified damage charges exceed the caution deposit, the outstanding balance will be requested from the guest.
  • Guests should report any pre-existing damage, stains, missing items, or defects shortly after arrival, preferably with photos or videos, for better clarity during the post-stay assessment.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in: 2:00 PM. Check-out: 12:00 PM. Unless your booking details state otherwise. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability, approval and any applicable fee. Extensions depend on availability, approval and payment, and are best requested at least 24 hours before check-out.

House rules

To keep every stay comfortable for guests and neighbours:

  • Registered and approved guests only.
  • Standard occupancy is maximum of 2 guests per room.
  • Additional overnight guests are not allowed and will require prior approval.
  • No parties, events, gatherings or overcrowding.
  • Please keep noise considerate for neighbours and building occupants.
  • Smoking is strictly prohibited inside all apartments. Smoking is only permitted on the balcony of apartments with balconies or in designated smoking areas, where provided. For apartments without a balcony or designated smoking area, smoking is not permitted anywhere on the premises.
  • No illegal activity or prohibited substances.
  • No pets unless approved.
  • No commercial filming, photoshoots or events without approval.
  • Please do not move, remove, damage or alter furniture, fixtures, appliances or other items.
  • Please follow building, estate or facility rules.
  • Please treat the apartment and its contents with care.
  • Please take care of your belongings, we are not liable for any missing or damaged item.

Property-specific rules: Will be communicated if any.

Before you pay

  • Payment validates reservation. An invoice is only valid within the hour. Kindly reconfirm availability before making payment after an hour of receiving the invoice.
  • Your reservation is confirmed once payment is received and Shortlet Magnificent confirms the booking in writing.
  • Please review the apartment details, location, dates, duration, number of guests, price and the terms on the invoice before making payment.
  • A valid means of identification will be required for booking reference and verification purposes.
